### Return Eligibility

You may request a return or replacement if your product is damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete, or where otherwise required by applicable law.

Change-of-mind returns, if offered, are subject to the eligibility conditions and return window communicated on the product page or at checkout.

### Return Conditions

For eligible non-defective returns, products should be unused, complete, and returned with their original accessories and packaging where required.

Certain electronic components, sensors, modules, and robotics kits may have specific return conditions, which will be communicated before purchase. These conditions do not restrict statutory consumer rights.

### How to Request a Return

Return requests can only be submitted through your account on the STEM ROBO HUB website. Go to My Account → My Orders, select the relevant order, and click Request Return. Provide the reason for your return and upload supporting photographs or videos, if required.

Our team will review your request and notify you of the next steps through your account.

### Replacements

Eligible replacements are subject to product availability and the nature of the issue. We may arrange inspection, troubleshooting, repair, replacement, or another appropriate remedy.
